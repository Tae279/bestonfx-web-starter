# International Forex Broker Market Compliance

## Scope

Use market-standard broker behavior for Thailand-facing Forex/CFD sites:

- Primary conversion CTA can be `เปิดบัญชี`.
- Secondary support CTA should be `ทัก LINE OA ติดต่อ admin`.
- Do not use a public deposit CTA until the funding flow, entity, and payment terms are approved.
- Thai SEC is not the controlling frame for this POC unless founder/legal changes scope.
- Use international regulator/entity proof only when the exact entity, regulator, and license number are approved.

## Required Risk Disclosure

Use a plain high-risk disclosure near hero CTAs, account tables, market pages, partner pages, tools, and footer:

```text
Forex/CFD และ Leverage มีความเสี่ยงสูง อาจทำให้สูญเสียเงินลงทุน โปรดศึกษาข้อมูลและความเสี่ยงก่อนตัดสินใจ
```

## Banned Copy

- Guaranteed profit or income
- Risk-free, low-risk, or no-loss language
- Guaranteed IB commission or partner earnings
- Guaranteed signal accuracy or win-rate claims
- Best / number one / top-ranking claims without source, date, geography, and approval
- Fake testimonials, fake review scores, fake trader counts, fake country counts
- Regulatory/license claims not approved by founder/legal/compliance
- Deposit-now urgency before account/regulator/funding flow is approved

## Safe Copy Pattern

Prefer:

- international regulator proof slots
- transparent account conditions
- platform/security/support process
- education and risk management
- LINE admin support before account opening
- placeholders for unconfirmed terms

Placeholder:

```text
รอยืนยันข้อมูลจากฝ่ายกำกับดูแลก่อนเผยแพร่
```

## Review Workflow

1. Identify every claim: regulation, entity, account terms, spread, leverage, commission, payout, support SLA, platform, tool, DX Trade, DX Academy, DX Exclusive, IB.
2. Mark each claim as `approved`, `needs source`, or `remove`.
3. Replace unapproved facts with the standard placeholder.
4. Keep `เปิดบัญชี` and `ทัก LINE OA ติดต่อ admin` as the two primary public CTAs.
5. Remove public deposit CTAs unless founder confirms funding URL and allowed wording.
6. Add the high-risk disclosure near claim-bearing conversion areas and in the footer.
7. Remove copy that frames trading as wealth growth, financial freedom, easy money, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Reference Behavior

- Exness Thailand uses international regulator framing, account/security proof, and risk warnings.
- XM Thailand uses `เปิดบัญชี` CTAs with risk warnings near conversion points.
- IUX Thailand/global uses Open Account CTA, regulator/license pages, execution/security proof, and CFD risk warnings.
